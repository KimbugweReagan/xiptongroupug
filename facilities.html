Where It Happens

Built for consistency, from intake to dispatch

Our processing site and dairy estate in Mbale are set up so that every batch of coffee and every litre of milk moves through the same controlled, traceable steps.

From covered drying beds to a hygienic milking area, each part of the facility exists to protect quality at the point it’s most fragile.

Grading & Storage

Dairy Estate

Quality Control

How We Protect Every Batch

  • 01

    Intake inspection

    Incoming coffee and milk are checked against baseline quality criteria before acceptance.

  • 02

    Controlled processing

    Drying, sorting and hygiene steps follow written procedures, not guesswork.

  • 03

    Grading & documentation

    Each batch is graded and logged, keeping a clear record back to its source farm.

  • 04

    Storage & dispatch checks

    A final check before goods leave the facility, protecting quality all the way to the buyer.

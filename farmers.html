Getting Started

How to Become a Partner Farmer

  • Step 1

    Reach out

    Contact our field team to introduce your farm or cooperative and the produce you grow.

  • Step 2

    Farm visit & onboarding

    A short visit to understand your setup, followed by onboarding into our farmer network.

  • Step 3

    Training & standards

    We walk through post-harvest handling or herd-management practices that meet our quality standards.

  • Step 4

    Regular collection begins

    Your coffee parchment or milk is collected on an agreed schedule at fair, transparent prices.
